# Tickrr: from market directory to intelligence product

The valuable output is a traceable answer to “What differs, why might it differ, and can I rely on it?” More generated prose does not establish an edge.

## This release

Free diagnostics cover comparable 24-hour repricing, bid–ask friction, requested-size depth and available margin-removed sportsbook disagreement. Manifold adds binary play-money crowd forecasts through two bounded searches and up to four full-market requests, a five-minute source cache and a shared request budget. Source failures remain explicit.

Related-question discovery flags numeric, possible negation, deadline and timestamp differences. Identical titles do not verify resolution equivalence. No cross-platform average or executable arbitrage claim is produced. OpenAI Luna assesses the retrieved evidence and computed diagnostics, with source links visible in the UI.

This is additional crowd context, not a second real-money order book. OpticOdds still needs an account and appropriate display/data rights. Kalshi is not connected. Forecasting superiority is not established.

## Next launch slice

Start with pre-match moneylines in one of the leagues already supported by the OpticOdds adapter, chosen by actual licensed coverage. Expand globally after measuring coverage and reliability.

1. **Licensed prices:** connect the existing OpticOdds account, or select a provider after checking coverage, delay, display rights, downstream redistribution rights and cost. Keep books distinct even when delivered by the same vendor; their forecasts are correlated evidence.
2. **Canonical events:** normalize competitor IDs, league, UTC start, period, market, selection, draw/overtime behavior, thresholds and settlement source. Store reviewed mappings and rejected/uncertain matches. Similarity proposes candidates; rule checks establish comparability.
3. **Executable comparisons:** synchronized quotes, fees/commission, liquidity at user size and quote age. Missing fees, limits or rule equivalence suppress executable-edge claims.
4. **Independent event evidence:** attach injuries and lineups to exact event/competitor IDs, publication and retrieval times. Surface corrections. Official event reports and licensed structured data should determine status; a model's guess cannot.
5. **Signal history and alerts:** persist frozen evidence, calculation versions, expiry and retraction reasons. Deduplicate meaningful changes. Notifications require explicit user opt-in.
6. **Proof:** record signal-time inputs, realistic paper fill assumptions, fees, closing line and resolution. Use chronological holdouts against bookmaker consensus and simple baselines. Report coverage, sample size, calibration/Brier score, closing-line value and after-cost paper performance separately. Backtests are not live returns.

## Launch gates

| Gate | Evidence required |
|---|---|
| Coverage | Measured league/book coverage and delays, with missing sources visible |
| Matching | Reviewed event and settlement mappings; false and uncertain matches fail closed |
| Arithmetic | Tests for two/three-way markets, margin removal, fees, stale quotes and partial fills |
| Provenance | Source ID, publication/retrieval time and calculation version for every finding |
| Pro value | Reviewed complete, conflicting, stale and missing-evidence cases, including abstention |
| Reliability | Outages never become agreement; bounded costs and disclosed cache age |
| Distribution | Consumer display and downstream API rights checked independently |

A research beta can launch in days. A credible claim of superior predictive performance requires a longer evaluation period.

## Source decisions — September 9, 2026

- [Manifold API](https://docs.manifold.markets/api): public search/full-market endpoints; this integration uses MANA binary forecasts as labeled context, not model-training data or a bulk-resale feed.
- [Kalshi Developer Agreement](https://kalshi-public-docs.s3.amazonaws.com/Kalshi-Developer-Agreement.pdf), section 3: obtain written authorization for aggregation/sharing beyond personal trading, or contract with an authorized distributor, before enabling customer comparisons. Public endpoints alone do not grant redistribution rights.
- [OpticOdds](https://developer.opticodds.com/): existing adapter supports odds, fixtures, injuries and lineups, subject to actual subscription entitlements and rights.

## Target experience

An event inbox: what changed → verified comparisons → why it matters → what invalidates it. Keep provenance and uncertainty free. Charge for deeper evidence history, alerts and research workflows. The present market list is discovery while the licensed event registry is built.
